## 1. The change (`exp/join-wave-base` `0e1ba4e`, one binary, `NICE_EXP_JOIN_WB` → comptime)

Host: `JoinDevice` reads `NICE_EXP_JOIN_WB` (0 = the base kernel) and passes it to `join_kernel` as a comptime `wb`;
bit 8 also goes to `bucket_kernel` as a comptime `pc_pre`. TT's bit is dropped on the host unless the runtime is
CUDA with 32-lane planes (`plane_size_min = plane_size_max = 32`, `Plane::Ops`), so lavapipe (8-lane) and every
other runtime keep the shared-memory copy. The wave's six arrays are now declared per work item (outside the batch
loop) in every variant, which WH needs; with WB = 0 the kernel compiles to the base's (§2).

| bit | candidate (L41 §5) | what the kernel does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **TT** (5): the transposed copy as a plane shuffle transpose | The batch gather keeps each thread's top's mask words (`glo`, `ghi`) in registers. Plane p's lanes hold chunk p's 32 tops (lane = `UNIT_POS_X % 32` on CUDA), so the chunk's digit-major words are the plane's 32×32 bit transpose. `bit_transpose32`: five butterfly stages (s = 16, 8, 4, 2, 1), each `plane_shuffle_xor(v, s)` and a swap of the off-diagonal s-blocks (`select(lane & s == 0, (v & m) \| ((y << s) & !m), (v & !m) \| ((y >> s) & m))`); lane d stores `t_tr[64c + d]` and `t_tr[64c + 32 + d]`. Lanes past the batch hold 0, so bits and chunks past it are 0 as before. One `sync_cube` per batch instead of two (nothing reads another thread's `t_lo`/`t_hi` before the AND); batches below `TRANSPOSE_MIN` skip the transpose as before. |
| 16 | **TT2** (with 1): a cheaper stage | Each lane sends a pre-shifted word (`select(low, v >> s, v << s)`) and keeps its half with one masked merge (`((v ^ y) & keep) ^ y`, `keep = select(low, m, !m)`); verified against the transpose on random 32×32 matrices on the host before building, like the first form. |
| 2 | **WH** (7): the wave load out of the batch loop | A work item whose bucket is one wave (`ne ≤ 512`) loads `mlo`, `mhi`, `rr`, `vv`, `ac`, `pc` in its first top batch only (`skip = ne <= wave && tc != ts`). |
| 4 | **AU** (8): the AND's digit loop at a fixed trip count | Per entry slot: `au_trips` = 2k trips (a runtime kernel argument; nvcc unrolls the loop ×4 with a remainder loop), no `(x \| y) != 0` test. A lane with an empty slot starts at `it = au_trips` and runs none, so a plane of empty slots skips the loop as on the base. |
| 8 | **PC** (9): `pc` in the bucket kernel | Where `pc` depends on the residue alone (`pc_pre_shape`: key level, no `b^(k−1)` term, no partition digits in `R' mod M`; true at the production shape k = 6, k2 = 8, f0 = 3), `bucket_kernel` writes `pc = 2ρ \| 3ρ² << 16` (ρ = r0 mod b^mid) to `ext_p[slot·nbp + i]`, and the join's wave load reads it instead of forming ρ, p2, p3 (17 instructions per slot-wave). `ext_p` is `max_slots · nbp` words, allocated only with the bit on and outside the plan's footprint (experimental). |

**AU's invariant** (the brief: verify the mask's popcount before relying on it). Every entry the bucket kernel lists
has exactly 2k digits in its mask by the time the AND reads it, at key level and otherwise:

- `bpre` (`Base::bot_dfs`): a residue mod b^f0 enters with 2·f0 mask bits: at each of its f0 positions the n² and
  n³ digits are distinct (`g2 == g3` is skipped) and new (`mask & (m2 | m3) != 0` is skipped). The prune by `fc` (and
  its fallback that prunes nothing) drops residues, never bits.
- `bucket_kernel`: each of the pp partition positions clears `ok` unless its two digits are distinct and miss `m | fc`,
  then ORs them in; only `ok` entries are listed (dead ones get `DEAD_MASK` and no list slot). A listed entry has
  2·(f0 + pp) bits.
- `JoinParams::supported` requires o − p ≤ 1. Not key level: o = p, k = f0 + pp: 2k bits. Key level (o − p = 1, k =
  f0 + pp + 1): the entry is listed under key d only if the key position's two digits are distinct and miss `mf = m |
  fc`, and the join kernel ORs in exactly those two digits (its g2, g3 are digit k − 1 of R² and R³ for R = R' +
  d·b^(k−1), the quantity the bucket kernel stepped as (u2 + d·s2, u3 + d·s3) mod b): 2k bits.
- So the trip count needs no gate. A wrong count would change the AND's pass bits and with them `and_count`; every
  parity test and every timed field kept the exact counts with AU on.

## 6. Against L41's estimate, and why AU and PC lose

| trim | L41: % of the join's instructions | L41: s per b62 1e17 field (150 W) | measured, screen (180 W) | per round | cycles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TT (TT2) | −3.5 | 0 to −1.7 | **−1.28 (−1.36) s** | ×1.012 (×1.013) | ×1.026 |
| WH | −1.3 | −0.4 + the list → entry latency (unmeasured) | **−1.61 s** | ×1.015 | ×1.023 |
| AU | −1.1 | ~−0.5 | **+0.50 s** | ×0.995 | ×1.018 |
| PC | −0.5 | ~−0.1 | **+0.78 s** | ×0.993 | ×1.004 |
| all four (31) | ≈ −6.4 | the brief: ≈ 3–4 % of the field | −1.87 s | ×1.018 | ×1.018 |
| TT2 + WH (19): `scr2` / A/B | ≈ −4.8 | | −2.45 s / −1.82 s | ×1.023 / ×1.017 | ×1.032 / ×1.016 |

- **TT** landed at the top of L41's time range. Its instruction cut per thread-batch (−143 / −168, a barrier) is
  ~75–90 % of L41's −190; TT2's 25 fewer instructions per thread-batch did not show in time.
- **WH** saved four times what its instruction share predicts (−1.61 s against ~−0.4 s). Skipping a wave load also
  skips its dependent `lists` → `bp_r`/`ext_m`/`ext_q` gathers, which L39 left as the wave's unexplained cost; the
  excess over the instruction share (~1.2 s) is consistent with that latency being real (inferred, not separately
  measured).
- **AU** cuts 3.25 instructions per digit but the AND phase is limited by its shared-memory loads, not its ALU work
  (L41 §2: 8 SM-cycles of MIO per digit against 5.5 of ALU; L37 priced the whole AND at 5 % of the join, 0–2 % in
  cycles). Unrolled ×4, the loop issues its loads denser and draws more power: in cycles it gains at b62 (×1.018), but
  the cap takes ~30 MHz and the time does not move (×0.995 / ×1.000).
- **PC** removes ~16 ALU instructions per slot-wave and adds a dependent 4-byte global load per slot-wave from a new
  per-(slot, entry) array, plus one store per entry per slot in the bucket kernel. The wave load is latency-bound
  (L39; WH's result above agrees), so a load on its chain costs more than the arithmetic it replaces: ×0.993 /
  ×0.994, neutral in cycles. (A host-side table of `pc` per `bpre` residue, `nbp` words shared by every slot, would
  avoid the per-slot array and the bucket kernel's store; not built.)
- **The kill line:** the brief parks below 3 % at b62 1e17. The best combination is ×1.017 in the A/B (×1.023 in its
  screen), so **park**. This was the last unmeasured part of the join: the per-wave base outside the walk is worth
  ~2 % of the field at b60/b62 by these four trims (the best two), 4–6 % at b64 (cycles / time).
