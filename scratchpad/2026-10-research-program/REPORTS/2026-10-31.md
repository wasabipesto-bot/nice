  fix uses the 16-bit-limb construction from #225, removes every flagged site from the generated
  WGSL of all 97 dumped kernels, is exact on CUDA and lavapipe with identical survivor and check
  counts, and costs nothing measurable. Everything else (bucket, join and check kernels, stride
  enumeration, per-number detailed kernel, browser kernel) keeps its remainders loop-carried
  and is clean; the stride prefilter at bases ≤ 40 has the shape in a form that passed an M4
  probe in August.
