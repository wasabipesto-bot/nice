## Addendum, 09:45 UTC: a fifth of base 62's real work is being done twice

**Found by today's audit, and it is the most valuable item on this page.** In base 62's first
nine hours, 126 of 530 real fields were searched twice: 19 % of the real submissions and 18 % of
the GPU time. 96 of the 126 were demoux both times, a median 38 minutes apart. The second
searches agree with the first, so this is waste, not an integrity problem.

- **Cause (read in the server's code):** the API pre-claims nice-only fields 200 at a time and
  hands them out in order with no check of their age, while a claim expires after one hour. At
  base 62 a batch lasts longer than an hour, so the next refill claims the back of the previous
  batch again. I had flagged this as a risk on 10-09 (backlog item 1); base 62's slow fields
  now trigger it.
- **Worth:** about ×1.2 of the whole search from now on, on every device, for a server deploy.
  That is as much as the three best open kernel PRs together.
- **What I am doing:** a fix as a PR against the API code, with a test that reproduces it,
  then an independent review. Expected this afternoon. I do not touch the server; deploying is
  yours.

**The audit itself is clean.**
- All 8,665 "instantly empty" verdicts are proved empty by an independent exact check.
- 156 re-run comparisons, no mismatch: 91 exact equalities (demoux), 65 within the known bound
  (the fleet's v3.4.7).
- **Main is ×3.13 faster than v3.4.7 at base 62** on an RTX 3080 (five fields, two rounds). If
  that carries over, the fleet's 338 s a field becomes about 108 s (decision 3).
- **Base 62 ends about 2027-01-06 as things are**, 2026-12-26 without the duplicates, a few
  days earlier again with the fleet on main. My morning range (30–41 days) did not know about
  the duplicates.
