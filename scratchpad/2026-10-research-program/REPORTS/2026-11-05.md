## What moved

- **Two join-kernel leads measured, neither opened as a PR.**
  - **Bottom-entry record (the bottom analogue of #241): neutral, closed.** Packing an entry's
    mask, prefilter words and residue into one record cuts the gather's memory sectors from 2.5
    to 1.0 per entry, measured on the production bucket lists, yet field time does not move

    warps. The ablation's 10.5 s for entry loads was an exposed load chain in a stripped kernel,
    which is a lesson for reading the rest of that breakdown: stripped-kernel costs are upper
    bounds.
  - **Group walk over the AND group's eight pass words: ×1.056 at b62 1e17 and ×1.074 at b60,
    exact, but parked.** Rounds per group fall ×0.65 and lane utilisation rises from 25% to
    38%. The cost is on small buckets: batches under 192 tops save no rounds and pay the larger
    round body, so the b58 MSD-strong NiceMark scenario is ×0.963 and 1e14 fields lose 3–4.5%,
    failing the "no scenario below ×0.98" rule. Five gating variants either spilled registers
    (two walks in one kernel) or stayed under ×0.98 there. Clock-normalised the production gain is
    ×1.10–1.12, because the kernel does more work per cycle and the capped card clocks down.
