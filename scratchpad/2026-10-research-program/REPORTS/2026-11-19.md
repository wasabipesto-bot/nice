## Addendum, 11:50 UTC: the morning's four pieces and one more

**The field structure (new research).** An independent review could not show that fields keyed by middle
digits are the wrong commitment, and proved two things that answer your concern directly:

- any search that splits n into a top part and a bottom part can restrict itself to such a field (today's
  overlap join too, about ×1.9 slower there);
- any field that fixes digits within positions 4–8 is a union of whole slices under any join shape, so fields
  can be re-cut later without redoing any search.

What the choice of fixed digits can cost is bounded at ×1.15 (base 64) and ×1.30 (base 65). It recommended
fixing digits 5–7 instead of 6–8; that is built, proven slice by slice against the old definition, and
measured at −3.1 % (the review had derived about 3 %).
