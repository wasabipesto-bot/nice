## What moved

- **The check kernel redesign is built, reviewed and PR-ready** (`pr/check-d`, on the FD round 2
  branch): n in base-b² limbs from the record's P mod b⁵ word, n² by u32 schoolbook, digits read
  off the carry normalisation with magic multiplies, a gate on n² and ten low digits of n³ before
  the rest of n³, and a device-side work counter in place of the fixed 1024-cube grid. ×1.088 at
  b62 1e17, ×1.124 at b60, ×1.070 at b64, NiceMark ×1.056 over six rounds. The check now runs at
  the card's DRAM floor (3.2 s per b62 1e17 field, 98% of bandwidth) with 39 registers and no
  divisions. Because this kernel decides hits and the old suite's hit comparisons were empty
  lists except b10's 69, it ships with new device tests comparing every survivor's digit count,
  gate result and mask against the CPU: 1.17 M real survivors and 85 k synthetic numbers over 22
  shapes, identical on CUDA and on lavapipe with both compilers. The reviewer modelled the kernel
  independently in Python with overflow and quotient-exactness checks, matched big-integer digits
  on all 20 production bands, and found no defect; its fixes (the test must notice a silently
  dropped batch; band-edge numbers at every shape; a gated test mode) are in.
- **The full stack profiled and retuned:** no kernel constant moves (every retune under 3%). But
  three new items: #249's host-side sort runs on the device thread with the GPU idle (3.2–3.6%
  of a b62 1e17 field, host-only, exact to move), one in seven runtime compiles of the join
  kernel picks a spilling register variant worth about 3% (a register pin fixes it; from now on
  A/B runs record or pin the variant), and the transposed AND, now 15% of the field, carries
  unfolded address arithmetic in 8 of its 30 instructions per digit. Group walk, compaction, fewer
  AND digits and a digit-major AND are all no-go by arithmetic.
- **The CPU join has two more measured levers:** a two-pass scan that removes the coin-toss
  branch on survivor extraction (×1.16–1.20, more with the survivor code inlined) and a check
  seeded with the digits the join already knows (×1.07–1.11 on top), ×1.32–1.39 together at
  production shapes, exact. The two-pass scan is being made into a PR branch now; the seeded
  check follows and can reuse the GPU check's arithmetic.
