# Research report, 2026-11-09 (day 13)

## What moved

- **#249 got a third commit:** its host-side counting sort of the top layer ran on the thread that
  drives the GPU, after the previous slice's batches had been read back, so the card idled
  through eleven sorts per b62 1e17 field. The sort now runs in the field plan for slice 0 and on
  the existing prep thread for later slices, and steps each prefix's residues from the previous
  prefix instead of dividing. Output word-for-word identical on every slice of four fields
  (30.6 M prefixes); ×1.022 at b62 1e17 and ×1.016 at b60 by same-process alternation (a
  separate-process A/B cannot resolve 2% under ±4% clock scatter). The two held GPU branches are
  rebased onto it; #249's body and a comment describe the commit.
- **The AND's codegen fold works but does not pay under a power cap:** the index fold cuts the
  AND loop from 30 to 20 instructions per digit (28% less issue in the AND), is exact, and buys
  only ×1.008–1.018 because denser issue lowers the clock. Parked as a ready rider. The register
  pin for the one-in-seven spilling compile cannot be expressed in CubeCL at all; the agent wrote
  the 25-line upstream patch (`min_cubes_per_sm` → `__launch_bounds__(256, 5)`) and traced the
  lottery to CubeCL placing shared arrays in randomised hash order.
- **The CPU seeded check is PR-ready and under review:** the full check of a survivor starts from
  the digits already proved (the bottom's listed digits, the prefilter's four, and the top's
  pre-closure certificate digits where the seed flag is set, never the closure-forced ones) and
  converts only the middle digits in base-b² columns. ×1.11–1.14 at production shapes at 1 and
  4 threads, NiceMark CPU ×1.155; the check's share of a partition falls from 15–16% to 5%. Exact
  position by position on 19.65 M survivors, with the column digits checked against a big-integer
  library on 275 k numbers and 298 k verdicts on adversarial numbers including b10's 69.
- Nothing merged since 3 November; no comments. Five 7-day worktrees cleared.
