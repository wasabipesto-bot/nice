## What moved

- **Demoux's telemetry on the merged build (your invitation): ×2.27 per b60 1e16 field** against
  the pre-stack main it ran the same morning (processing 32.2 s → 14.2 s; about 7.0e14 numbers per
  second), 1,842 fields by 00:47 your time, none disqualified, none found. That is more than the
  3080's ×1.80 at b60, so the AMD/SPIR-V path gained at least as much as CUDA.
- **The hourly bursts of ~50 instant zero-work submissions are not a defect.** They are
  consecutive fields whose own certificate repeats a digit: 149 of 160 confirmed by recomputing
  the certificate from the field ranges (none of 840 normal fields), the other 11 are eliminated
  range by range (0–2 of 7,200 sub-ranges survive the per-range certificate, against 5,209 for a
  normal field). The fleet, mayalaran and your October builds show the same bursts.
- **The join kernel's cost breakdown on main, by ablation** (the phase timers could not split
  it): the AND is 5% of the join after #239; survivor handling is 54% (the prefilter alone 33%,
  the walk 16%, the stage 5%) at 25% lane utilisation; and **gathering each batch's tops into
  shared memory is 26% of the join, 14% of the field**, almost all of it on two of the four
  separate top arrays. The residue test in the walk never rejects in production.
- **Two prototypes killed, both exact but slower:** compacting the walk across the warp
  (×0.94; the round body grows more than the rounds shrink, and the card clocks down) and the
  warp-shuffle register table for the AND (×0.95; shuffles use the same pipe as the shared loads).
  A simpler variant of the first, one walk per lane over both entry slots, is ×1.015–1.026 and
  parked.
- **Lead in flight (L38): one 32-byte record per top.** The join reads a top's words from three
  separate arrays at a random index, touching three or four 32-byte sectors for 24 bytes of
  payload, 7.6e10 times per field. One record per top makes that one sector. Estimate 9–12%
  at b62 1e17 and b60 1e16; the agent is building and measuring it now (hard stop 13:30 UTC).
  If it clears the bar, the PR branch will be ready and I open it this afternoon.
