Overlap join: sort the top layer off the device thread

JoinDevice::new sorted each slice's top layer by group (the static
bucket order's counting sort) on the thread that drives the device,
after the previous slice's last read and before this slice's first
launch, so the device sat idle through it: 2.3 s of a b62 1e17 field
(11 slices) and 0.4 s of a b60 1e16 field on a 2.2 GHz Broadwell host.
The thread that prepares the next slice's setup while the current one
runs did not sort it.

- sorted_layer moves to join_plan and the device stage takes the layer
  ready: plan_join sorts the first slice's (the client plans a field
  beside the device's work on the previous one), and the thread that
  prepares each later slice's setup sorts that slice's.
- The layer is ascending and its prefixes mostly a few apart (more
  than 99.8 % of steps are below b on the fields measured), so each
  prefix's residues mod b, b - 1 and b^(k2 - f0 - p) come from the
  previous prefix's by adding the difference; u64 divisions are left
  for the larger gaps, and the class mod b - 1 is a table lookup. The
  sort takes half the time it did; the rest is the scatter.
- The same words and offsets: the host test pins them, now also on a
  reversed layer, where every residue comes from a division.

x1.022 at b62 1e17 and x1.016 at b60 1e16 (RTX 3080 at 150 W).
