## Summary

- **Where the join goes on main** (b62 1e17, profiled, 3 interleaved rounds; marginal costs adding one piece at a
  time; b60 1e16 in brackets): join **72.4 s = 55.8 % of the 129.7 s field** (59 %).

  | part | s/field | % of join | % of field |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | base (no AND: per work item the top batch, the entry loads and prefilter terms, the transposed copy, the per-chunk stage check) | 29.7 (5.7) | **41.0 %** (37.9) | 22.9 % (22.4) |
  | AND (the transposed digit ORs, #239's loop) | 3.7 (0.7) | **5.1 %** (4.4) | 2.8 % (2.6) |
  | walk (rounds, set-bit pops, residue test) | 11.3 (2.3) | **15.6 %** (15.5) | 8.7 % (9.1) |
  | prefilter (`walk_pf`) | 24.2 (5.7) | **33.4 %** (37.6) | 18.6 % (22.2) |
  | stage + flush | 3.6 (0.7) | **4.9 %** (4.7) | 2.7 % (2.7) |

  - **Survivor handling (walk + prefilter + stage) is 54 % of the join, 30 % of the field**; the AND is 5 % (0–2 %
    clock-normalised): the brief's 40–45 % "AND phase" was mostly the base.
  - **Inside the base** (modes 9–14): **gathering the batch's tops into shared memory is 18.6 s, 26 % of the join
    (14 % of the field)**, and 15.7 s of it is the second and third of the three top arrays (`top_r`, `top_x`): `tl`
    alone costs 0.35 s, `top_m` alone 2.5 s. The entry loads and prefilter terms are 10.5 s (14 %); the transposed copy,
    the per-chunk stage check (two barriers per chunk), work items, barriers and atomics together ~1 s.
  - **Lane utilisation of the walk: 24.8 %** (b60 23.5 %): 1.28·10^11 plane-rounds per field, 3.36 per (plane, entry
    slot, chunk) (4.45), 0.84 pass bits per lane-chunk (1.29 per lane holding an entry). The survivor path is
    issue-bound per round (~16 SM-cycles of `walk_pf` per round ≈ its 52 SASS instructions at 4 per cycle).
  - **The residue test never rejects in production:** the AND's pass bits equal the in-range survivors (to 4 in
    1.02·10^12).
  - **#239 re-measured on main:** the per-top AND instead of the transposed one costs +12.5 s of join, **×1.095** on
    the b62 1e17 field (b60 ×1.072).
- **H1, walk compaction (the brief's design): exact, slower, killed.** One binary, `NICE_EXP_JOIN_COMPACT=1`, 3 + 2
  interleaved rounds: **b62 1e17 ×0.940, b62 1e16 ×0.937, b60 1e16 ×0.962, b64 1e16 ×0.912, NiceMark ×0.923**
  (MSD-strong ×0.834). 48 registers + 32 B spills (main 40, none), 5 cubes/SM either way. Exactness: CPU reference incl.
  b50/b54, both dense fields, whole b42, the 1e14 compare at b57/60/62/64 (main's counts), every A/B field, lavapipe
  naga + direct SPIR-V (8-lane subgroups), WGSL lint 0 flagged.
  - Why: at 1.3 pass bits per lane-chunk the compaction only takes a full plane-slot from ~4.1 rounds to ~1.9, while its
    round body is 172 SASS instructions against main's 110 (12 shuffles in two dependent chains + a 5-step bit select),
    and every slot-chunk pays CubeCL's `plane_inclusive_sum` (a runtime `SHFL.UP` loop with a 32-bit remainder, ~50
    instructions) even when empty. In cycles it is ×1.01–1.08 *faster*, but it draws more power per cycle and runs
    70–140 MHz lower at the 150 W cap.
  - **Variant `COMPACT=2`** (one walk per lane over both entry slots, no shuffles; 45 registers): exact (the same list),
    **×1.015 / ×1.014 / ×1.026 / ×1.012, NiceMark ×1.017** (3 rounds; b60 MSD-weak ×0.982, the 1e14 set ×0.99–1.02).
    A real but small gain, under the 5 % bar.
  - What could still pay (untested, the rule is to stop): compacting the AND group's 4 chunks × 2 slots at once with
    an unrolled scan and empty-plane skip; a rough count gives ~30 % off the survivor path (~10 % of the field).
- **H2, shuffle register LUT for the AND: exact, slower, killed.** `NICE_EXP_JOIN_SHFL=1`, 2 interleaved rounds:
  **b62 1e17 ×0.953, b60 1e16 ×0.969, b62 1e16 ×0.976; the AND-only ablation ×0.919 (b62 1e17) / ×0.918 (b60) /
  ×0.909 (b62 1e16)**; 47 registers; not a clock effect (same or higher clock, cycles ×0.94–0.95). Shuffles and shared loads share the MIO pipe at the same rate, and the two plane-uniform loops (low
  digits, high digits) run ~1.3–1.5× the rounds of main's per-lane loop; with the AND at 5 % of the join there was
  little to win anyway. Exactness: the same CUDA list, all pass with main's counts.
- **The box (59063596) can be destroyed:** every log is in `raw/box/`.
- **For a next brief:** (1) **the top record**: write each top's six words as one 32-byte record (`top_m`, `top_r`,
  `top_x` interleaved and padded) in the top kernels and read it in the join; the ablation puts 15.7 s of the 72.4 s
  b62 1e17 join on the two extra arrays, so a one-stream gather could save 12–16 s per field (9–12 % of the field)
  for a layout change (estimate, §1b). (2) **the survivor path** (54 % of the join at 25 % lane utilisation, issue-bound
  per round): a compaction over the AND group's 4 chunks × 2 slots with an unrolled scan and an empty-plane skip, or a
  cheaper `walk_pf` (four constant div/mods by b and two by b^mid per pair). (3) H1's mode 2 (+1–3 %, exact, 45
  registers) could ride along with either.

## 1. Breakdown of `join_kernel` on main by ablation

**Method.** Tree `A` (`exp/join-l37-ablate`): main + the `NICE_EXP_GPU_PROFILE` phase timers + a comptime ablation switch
`NICE_EXP_JOIN_ABL` (and `NICE_EXP_JOIN_TMIN` for the transposed-AND gate), one binary. Every run profiled (device
synchronised around each kernel group, so the join phase is the join kernel alone and is not disturbed by the check
kernel, whose input the ablations empty). `cubecl_cuda_join_throughput_fixed_fields` at L30's starts, b62 1e17 (1 field)
and b60 1e16 (2 fields, per-field means), **3 interleaved rounds** (each round: the six modes at b62, then at b60).
Modes, each a separate kernel (comptime):

- **c** full = main's kernel (40 registers, 1088 SASS instructions at b62, = H1's tree with its switch off).
- **a** AND only: the pass masks are formed as on main, then `and_count += popcount(pm)` (keeps them alive) and the walk
  is skipped.
- **b** walk with the residue test but no prefilter and no stage (`and_count` counts the in-range pairs).
- **p** walk and prefilter, no stage (prefilter passes counted in a register).
- **z** no AND: every pass mask 0; entries, tops, the transposed copy and the per-chunk stage check run as on main.
- **d** full with `TMIN = 2^30` (every batch takes the per-top AND, i.e. main without #239's transposed AND).
- In a, b and z an XOR checksum of every entry's loaded words (masks, carries, residue) is folded into a never-true
  compare, so the entry loads and the prefilter terms stay in the kernel.

The parts are **marginal costs in the order z → a → b → p → c** (each mode adds one piece to the previous one); in a
kernel that is part latency-bound a piece costs less added to a light kernel than it would cost removed from the full
one, so the "base" absorbs some overlap.

- Counts: mode c's survivors / checked are main's exact counts in all three rounds (b62 1e17 1,020,844,021,499 /

- **The base is the largest part: 41 % of the join, 23 % of the field** (29.7 s at b62 1e17): per bucket the work-item
  and top-list gathers, the top batch's six shared arrays and the transposed copy (two barriers), the entry loads

- **Survivor handling is 54 % of the join, 30 % of the field** (walk 16 %, prefilter 33 %, stage 5 % of the join at

- **The top loads dominate the base: 18.6 s, 26 % of the join, 14 % of the field** (11 − 12; b60 3.8 s, 25 %): per
  batch of up to 256 tops, thread u reads `tl[ts + u]` and gathers six words of top `t` from three arrays (`top_m`,
  `top_r`, `top_x`, two words each: three 32-byte sectors per top, from arrays of hundreds of MB that the 5 MB L2 cannot
  hold) into six shared arrays. Without them (mode 12) the work items, the batches' barriers and the cubes' atomics
  cost 1.1 s (1.5 % of the join). Each certified top is listed in `nroots` buckets (`top_fill_kernel`), so its words
  are gathered once per bucket that lists it.
- **And within them, the second and third arrays are the cost:** reading `tl` alone costs 0.35 s (14 − 12), the
  `top_m` gather (one sector per top) 2.5 s (13 − 14), the `top_r` and `top_x` gathers 15.7 s (11 − 13; b60: 0.11 /
  0.49 / 3.25 s). Two more sectors from two more arrays cost six times the first: not a sector count but the pattern,
  three independent random streams at the same index into three equal-sized arrays (DRAM row and TLB locality are the
  likely causes; this harness cannot tell which). One 32-byte record per top (the six words interleaved and padded)
  would make it one stream and one sector; if a record gather costs between mode 13's top_m gather (2.5 s) and a
  third of today's three (6.1 s), it saves 12–16 s of the 72.4 s join per b62 1e17 field, 9–12 % of the field
  (estimate; not built).
- **The entry loads and the key-level prefilter terms: 10.5 s, 14 % of the join** (9 − 10; b60 1.9 s, 13 %): the
  `lists` → `bp_r`/`ext_m`/`ext_q` gathers and the mod arithmetic forming `ac`/`pc` once per wave.
- **The transposed copy and the chunk loop cost nothing measurable** (4 − 9 and 10 − 11 are −0.2 and −0.3 s at b62
  1e17, within the rounds' spread): #239's copy is hidden behind the top loads, and the per-chunk stage check's two
  barriers are not a cost either (5 against 4 at b62 1e16: +3.8 %, single runs; checking every 4 chunks (7) 1.7 % less
  join, every 8 (8) 2.8 % more: noise-level and contradictory, so not pursued).

## 2. H1: walk compaction (`exp/join-walk-compact`, `NICE_EXP_JOIN_COMPACT`)

**Prototype** (one binary, host env → comptime; 0 = main's walk, verbatim):

- **1 = the brief's design.** Per entry slot and 32-top chunk: `x = pm & vv`, `cnt = popcount(x)`, `incl =
  plane_inclusive_sum(cnt)`, `excl = incl − cnt`, `total` = lane 31's `incl` (`plane_broadcast`); then rounds `r` while
  `32r < total`: item `32r + lane`; the owner = the number of lanes with `incl ≤ item`, by a 5-step binary search over
  `incl` with `plane_shuffle` (unrolled with the plane size 32 as a comptime constant on CUDA; a runtime loop over
  `PLANE_DIM` elsewhere); the owner's `x` and `excl` by shuffle; the owner's n-th set bit by a 5-step popcount search;
  the owner's `rr`, `ac`, `pc`, `mlo`, `mhi` by shuffle; then main's residue test, `walk_pf` and stage, with every lane
  of a round busy but the last. Exact by construction (the same pairs reach the same tests; only the stage order moves,
  which is atomic order already).
- **2 = a variant without shuffles:** one walk per lane over both entry slots' bits (`while plane_any((x0 | x1) != 0)`,
  a round pops from slot 0 while it has bits, else from slot 1), so a plane runs the max over lanes of the summed
  popcounts instead of the sum of the two maxima.

**Why the compaction loses** (main's and mode 1's SASS at b62, `raw/box/sass/C/`):

- The gain is small at this density. A plane-slot with entries in all 32 lanes has ~41 pass bits per chunk (Poisson
  1.29 per lane): main walks them in max-over-lanes ≈ 4.1 rounds, the compaction in ⌈T/32⌉ ≈ 1.9 (simulated). A plane-slot with
  few or no live lanes (the second slot is about a third full) gains nothing.
- Each compacted round costs much more: mode 1's round body is **172 SASS instructions** (`0x2fa0`–`0x3a60`) against
  main's 110 (19 vote/pop/residue + 52 `walk_pf` + 36 stage + 3 loop, `0x2ac0`–`0x3190`): 12 `SHFL.IDX` per round (5 for the
  owner search, 2 for the owner's mask and offset, 5 for its entry) in two dependent chains, plus the 5-step bit
  select (~25 ALU instructions).
- Each slot-chunk pays the prefix sum whether or not it has bits: CubeCL's `plane_inclusive_sum` compiles to a runtime
  loop of `SHFL.UP` over offsets up to `PLANE_DIM` with a full 32-bit remainder for the lane index (`I2F`/`MUFU.RCP`
  sequence), ~50 instructions, plus the broadcast; main pays one `VOTE.ANY` for an empty plane-slot.
- So on a full plane-slot the ~1.9 rounds' extra ~120 instructions plus the ~50 of the prefix about cancel the ~2.2
  rounds × ~75 executed instructions saved, and every sparse or empty plane-slot loses ~50; the spills add
  local-memory traffic on top. Lane utilisation goes up (to ~67 % on full slots) but the instructions per useful pair
  barely move; the measured cycles (×1.01–1.08) say the same, and the extra power per cycle at the 150 W cap turns
  that into a loss.
- What would have to change for compaction to pay (not built; the brief's rule is to stop): amortise the prefix sum
  and the rounds' fixed cost over more items per compaction — compact the AND group's 4 chunks × 2 slots at once
  (the transposed AND forms all 8 pass words together; ~165 items per plane → 6 rounds against main's ~4 × 4.1), skip
  plane-slots with no bits (one vote), and replace CubeCL's generic scan with an unrolled 5-step `shfl_up`. A rough
  count gives ~30 % off the survivor path (≈ 10 % of the field at b62 1e17) if the per-round overhead stays near 65
  instructions; untested.

## 3. H2: shuffle register LUT for the AND (`exp/join-shfl-lut`, `NICE_EXP_JOIN_SHFL`)

**Prototype** (on top of the ablation tree, so the AND-only ablation and H2 share one binary): at the start of each AND
group (4 chunks, the transposed path, batches of ≥ 192 tops) lane l loads `rlo[c] = t_tr[ab + 64c + l]` and `rhi[c] =
t_tr[ab + 64c + 32 + l]` for c = 0..3 (8 registers, conflict-free, consecutive lanes on consecutive words); per entry
slot, one plane-uniform loop over the entry's low digits (`acc[c] |= shfl(rlo[c], d)`) and one over its high digits
(`shfl(rhi[c], d − 32)`), 4 shuffles per round, lanes without a digit left masked by a select. CUDA only (the host
gates it on the runtime name: it needs 32-lane planes); the shared `t_tr` stays for the build and the register load,
so #239's 2 KiB of shared memory stays.

**Registers** (`raw/box/sass/S/registers.txt`): **47** (main 40), no spills, 1288 SASS instructions at b62 (main
1088), 25 `SHFL` (main 7). Still 5 cubes per SM.

  (×0.909–0.919).** Not a clock effect: H2 ran at the same or a higher clock (cycles ×0.94–0.95 on the fields). **Killed** (the
  brief: < 3 % on the AND-only ablation or any field slower).
- **Exact:** the same exactness list as H1 (CUDA, `x_S1_*.log`, `cmp_S1_e14.log`): CPU-reference parity incl. b50/b54
  ok (88 s), dense b58 / b62 ok (asserted equal to the CPU join), whole b42 ok, 1e14 compare at b57/60/62/64 ok with
  main's counts to the last digit; every confirmation field gives main's exact counts. (Lavapipe not run: H2 is gated

**Why** (SASS at b62, `raw/box/sass/S/`):

- **The ceiling was small:** the transposed digit ORs are ~5 % of the join (§1), so even a free AND could not reach
  the brief's expectation.
- **Per digit, a shuffle replaces a shared load on the same pipe:** main's AND iteration is 21 instructions with 4
  `LDS` (`0x2690`–`0x27d0`), H2's round 25 with 4 `SHFL.IDX` + 4 `SEL` (`0x2770`–`0x28f0`); `SHFL` and `LDS` both issue
  to the MIO pipe at 32 lanes per clock, so a 2-way-conflicted `LDS` (two wavefronts) becomes one `SHFL` at best, and
  the 4 selects and the extra vote are new.
- **More rounds:** main's digit loop is per lane (the plane runs max over lanes of the entry's ~12–14 digits, a lane
  that is done drops out); H2 needs every lane in each round, in two plane-uniform loops (low digits, then high
  digits), so a plane runs max(low) + max(high) rounds, by estimate 1.3–1.5× as many (round counts not measured).
  That puts the AND's instruction work at ~1.5–1.8× main's, in line with the AND-only ablation (+2.9 s on the AND's
  3.7 s at b62 1e17).
- In the full kernel the loss is larger than in the AND-only one (+6.3 s against +2.9 s at b62 1e17): with the walk
  present the SM is issue-bound (§1), so the AND's extra instructions take issue slots from the walk; in the
  AND-only kernel part of them hide behind the base's memory stalls.
