## 1. The change (`pr/join-group-walk`)

On the base, the join kernel's chunk loop (`jc` in steps of 32 tops) forms the pass words of an AND group of 4 chunks
× 2 entry slots once per group (the transposed AND, batches of ≥ `TRANSPOSE_MIN` = 192 tops) or one chunk's 2 words
(the per-top test, smaller batches), and then walks each chunk's words slot by slot: `while plane_any(x != 0)`, one
pass bit per lane per round. A plane runs the most bits one of its lanes has in each (slot, chunk) word, summed over the
words.

The branch changes only that loop (`common/src/cubecl_join.rs`, `join_kernel`):

- **Batches of ≥ 192 tops step by an AND group** (128 tops) and form the group's 8 pass words `pw[c·ept + j]` (chunk c,
  entry slot j) first: the same digit loop and ORs as the base (`t_tr[ab + d + 64c]`), then `pw = (pw ^ cm_c) & cm_c &
  vv_j`, `cm_c` masking chunk c to the batch's tops (0 for a chunk past the batch's end). `nm` = the non-empty words.
- **The group walk:** each round, a lane whose current word `x` is spent takes its next non-empty word (`wi` = the
  lowest bit of `nm`, cleared; `x = pw[wi]` by a 3-level tree of selects on `wi`'s bits; `jb = jc + (wi / ept)·32`).
  Then `jt2 = jb + ctz(x)`, `x &= x − 1`, the entry's registers (`rr`, `ac`, `pc`, `mlo`, `mhi`) of slot `wi % ept` by a
  select, and the base's round body unchanged: the residue test, `walk_pf`, the stage append, `sync_plane` on CUDA.
  The loop runs `while plane_any((x | nm) != 0)`, so a plane takes the most pass bits one lane has in the group.
- **Smaller batches step by one chunk** as before, with the base's per-top test into words 0..ept (the others stay 0);
  the lane's first word is picked right there by a select over its `ept` words (`wi` = lowest bit of `nm`; a lane
  with none keeps `x = nm = 0`), and the same walk follows, picking again only if the lane moves on to its second
  word. (`exp/join-group-walk-f2` instead walks a small batch's two words by L37's mode-2 loop, `while
  plane_any((x0 | x1) != 0)`, no pick, and lets the group walk exit at once; §3, §5.)
- **The stage check** (two cube barriers, flush at ≥ 512) runs once per group (per chunk in a small batch). A group's
  appends beyond the stage's 1024 go to the list directly, as a chunk's did; exact either way (L37 found no difference
  between checking every 4 or 8 chunks).
- **Nothing else moves:** the batch load, the transposed copy, the entry loads, `walk_pf`, the stage and flush code,
  the host side. The same (entry, top) pairs reach the same tests; only the order of the stage's atomic appends
  changes, which was never fixed.
- Kept compact for the direct SPIR-V compiler: one round body (the base had two, one per entry slot), the words in
  registers indexed only by unrolled constants (the pick is selects), no unrolled copies of the body.

**The residue test stays.** A top's range is `[a − pw0, e − pw0 + 1)` from `top_kernel` (clipped blocks, the field's
edges) and `[0, w)` from `top_fd_kernel`. The AND compares digit masks only, so it cannot see a clipped top's range: an
entry of the bucket can pass the AND against a clipped top and lie outside its range. L37's ablation counted it: pass
bits 1,020,844,021,504 against 1,020,844,021,499 in-range pairs at b62 1e17 (its mode a against main), so the test
rejects a handful of pairs per field, at the field's edges, and the CPU join has the same test. It costs 2 shared
loads and 2 compares per round; dropping it would change the survivor counts.

## 8. Verdict

- **The bar** (brief: ≥ 5 % at b62 1e17 and b60 1e16, no NiceMark scenario below ×0.98): b62 1e17 ×1.056 ✓, b60 1e16
  ×1.074 ✓, **NiceMark b58 MSD-strong ×0.968 (2 rounds) / ×0.963 (6 rounds) ✗**. Below the bar, so the branch is pushed
  as one squashed commit (`pr/join-group-walk` `f8a27cc`, CHANGELOG line with `#NNN`), and neither `PR-BODY.md` nor a PR.
- The same holds for every variant tried: `P` ×0.959, `F` ×0.935, `H` ×0.975 (screen), `F2` ×0.964 (6 A/B rounds);
  `H2`'s ×0.991 and `F2`'s ×1.020 were screens of 2–3 rounds and did not hold under the A/B.
- **What the change buys where it applies:** on the 1e16–1e17 fields (batches of 192–256 tops) the walk takes ~35–40 %
  fewer rounds at 38–41 % lane utilisation, for 5–8 % of the field at the 150 W cap and 6–12 % in cycles.
- **What it costs:** on fields of tiny buckets (MSD-strong, 1e14) nothing is saved and 1–5 % is lost; the variants
