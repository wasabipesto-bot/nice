## 1. The change

On the base, the join kernel's chunk loop (`jc` in steps of 32 tops) forms the pass words of an AND group of 4 chunks
× 2 entry slots once per group (the transposed AND, batches of ≥ 192 tops) or one chunk's 2 words per chunk (the
per-top AND, smaller batches), and then walks each chunk's words slot by slot: `while plane_any(x != 0)`, one pass bit
per lane per round. A plane runs the max over its lanes of each (slot, chunk) word's popcount, summed over the 8 words
of a group.

`pr/join-group-walk` changes only that loop (`common/src/cubecl_join.rs`, `join_kernel`):

- **The loop runs per AND group** (`jc` in steps of `AND_GROUP · 32` = 128 tops) and forms the group's 8 pass words
  `pw[c·ept + j]` (chunk c, entry slot j) first:
  - transposed AND: the same digit loop and ORs as the base (`t_tr[ab + d + 64c]`), then `pw = (pw ^ cm_c) & cm_c &
    vv_j`, where `cm_c` masks chunk c to the batch's tops (0 for a chunk past the batch's end, whose `t_tr` words are 0);
  - per-top AND: a runtime loop over the group's chunks (each the base's per-top loop over its ≤ 32 tops); the
    chunk's 2 words go into `pw` by a select on the chunk index (8 selects per chunk, no indexed registers).
- **One walk over the 8 words:** `nm` = the lane's non-empty words (one bit each). Each round, a lane whose current
  word `x` is spent takes its next non-empty word: `wi` = lowest bit of `nm`, cleared; `x = pw[wi]` by a 3-level tree of
  selects on `wi`'s bits; `jb = jc + (wi / ept)·32`. Then `jt2 = jb + ctz(x)`, `x &= x − 1`, the entry's registers
  (`rr`, `ac`, `pc`, `mlo`, `mhi`) of slot `wi % ept` by a select, and the base's round body unchanged: the residue
  test, `walk_pf`, the stage append, `sync_plane` on CUDA. The loop runs `while plane_any((x | nm) != 0)`, so a plane
  takes the max over its lanes of the lane's pass bits in the whole group.
- **The stage check** (two cube barriers, flush at ≥ 512) runs once per group instead of once per chunk; a group's
  appends beyond the stage's 1024 go to the list directly, as a chunk's did (exact either way; L37 found no difference
  between checking every 4 or 8 chunks).
- **Nothing else moves:** the batch load, the transposed copy, the entry loads, `walk_pf`, the stage and flush code,
  the host side. The same (entry, top) pairs reach the same tests; only the order of the stage's atomic appends
  changes, which was never fixed.
- Kept compact for the direct SPIR-V compiler: one round body, the 8 words in registers indexed only by unrolled
  constants (the pick is selects), no unrolled copies of the body.

**The residue test stays.** A top's range is `[a − pw0, e − pw0 + 1)` from `top_kernel` (clipped blocks, the field's
edges) and `[0, w)` from `top_fd_kernel`. The AND compares digit masks only, so it cannot see a clipped top's range: an
entry of the bucket can pass the AND against a clipped top and lie outside its range. L37's ablation counted that:
pass bits 1,020,844,021,504 against 1,020,844,021,499 in-range pairs at b62 1e17 (mode a against main), so the test
rejects a handful of pairs per field, all at the field's edges, and the CPU join has the same test. It costs 2 shared
loads and 2 compares per round (L37: never-taken branch); dropping it would change the survivor counts.

## 3. Rounds per group and lane utilisation (the walk counters)

**Counters** (`exp/join-rounds-base`, `exp/join-group-walk-rounds`; host env `NICE_EXP_JOIN_ROUNDS` → comptime; 0 = the
kernel unchanged): lane 0 of each plane adds 1 to the thread's `and_count` term per walk round (mode 1), per 32-top
chunk of a batch the plane visits (mode 2; the group walk adds the group's chunk count, `min(4, ⌈(nt − jc)/32⌉)`), or
per AND group (mode 3; the base counts a chunk with `(jc/32) % 4 == 0`), and the in-range survivor count is off. So the
harness's `survivors=` is plane-rounds, plane-chunks or plane-groups. Useful lane-rounds = the exact survivors (pass
bits in range; L37: = pass bits to within 5 in 1.02·10^12). One run per mode and side (b62 1e17 1 field, b60 1e16 2
fields, totals); `raw/ctr_table.md`:

  tops in every chunk, and a plane whose second entry slot is filled has about twice the bits of one whose second slot
  is empty (L37: the second slot is about a third full). The heaviest lane dominates every chunk, which the base
  already pays once per chunk; the group walk removes only the chunk-to-chunk and slot-to-slot variation.

- **Occupancy unchanged:** 48 × 256 × 5 = 61,440 ≤ 65,536 registers, so 5 cubes of 256 per SM, set by shared memory
  as on the base. 48 is the most that keeps 5 (ptxas allocates per warp in units of 8 registers per thread).
- **Walk loop at b62** (`raw/scripts/walkloop.py`): the base has two loops (one per entry slot) of **110**
  instructions each (71 up to the stage: vote, pop, residue test, `walk_pf`; 39 stage and tail; L37's count). `P` has
  **one loop of 139** (100 up to the stage, of which **19 are the word pick**, a branch taken in a round where any lane
  needs a new word; 39 stage and tail). So a round costs ~83 instructions outside the stage when no lane picks a word,
  ~102 when one does, against the base's 71; `G`'s linear pick made it 143 / 104. b60: base 108 (69), `P` 137 (98);
  b64: base 106/105 (64/63), `P` 130 (91).
- Rounds ×0.607 at ~1.2–1.4× the instructions per round: the walk's issued instructions fall by roughly a quarter
  (the stage's are per survivor and unchanged).
