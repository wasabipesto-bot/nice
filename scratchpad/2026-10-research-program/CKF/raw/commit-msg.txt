Overlap join: hand out check entries to whatever lanes run, and count them

The check kernel took its entries one plane at a time: lane 0 took
PLANE_DIM entries and lane i checked entry cur + i. That covers every
entry only on a full plane that has a lane 0. CUDA and Metal guarantee
full planes for 256-thread cubes; Vulkan does not (wgpu does not ask for
full subgroups). On a cube of 100 or 48 threads, where the last warp is
partly filled, the old kernel left 896 of 4,044 and 1,008 of 4,044
entries unchecked on CUDA, and nothing noticed.

- take_entries: the lanes that run a take decide it among themselves.
  With a ballot (planes of at most 32 lanes): its first word is the set of
  lanes taking part, so the count, each lane's rank and the leader (the
  lowest such lane) come from it, and the leader's counter value reaches
  the others by a shuffle from that lane. Without (wider planes): an
  exclusive scan and a sum of 1 over the active lanes, and the leader's
  value as a sum. No PLANE_DIM, no lane 0.
- The kernel counts the entries it checked and sums their indices into
  two more words of `ran`; the host compares both with the list's
  min(checked, list_cap) entries after every batch and re-run, and a
  mismatch is an error (the field fails), never fewer candidates.
- Tests: the check's count, gated and probe modes also on cubes that end
  in a partly filled plane and with the scan form; a new test of every
  shape on cubes of 100, 48 and 256 threads in both forms; a test-only
  mode that skips the first entry, which must fail the field in a batch
  pass and in a partition's re-run.
