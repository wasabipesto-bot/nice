Overlap join: append the FD kernel's tops without assuming lane 0

top_slot appended a plane's kept tops through lane 0: it tested lane 0's
group, let only lane 0 take the group's atomic and broadcast lane 0's
result. Vulkan does not promise full subgroups (wgpu does not ask for
them), and on a plane that runs without lane 0 no lane took the atomic:
the kept tops were written at offsets other planes also use and never
counted, so the join silently lost them.

- The leader is now the lowest kept lane of the lanes that run the call.
  Its group is tested, it alone takes the atomic when every kept lane is of
  that group, and the result reaches the others from it: by a shuffle from
  the ballot's lowest set bit (planes of at most 32 lanes), else as a sum
  over the active lanes in which only the leader's term is nonzero. A
  plane whose kept tops straddle groups still takes one atomic per kept
  lane. Same number of plane operations as before.
- The FD kernel keeps an account of its appends in two new words of `ran`:
  tops kept minus records reserved in grp_cnt, and the indices of the
  records written minus those reserved (mod 2^32), summed per cube in
  shared memory. The host requires both to be zero after every batch, in
  run_pass and in run_partition (top_complete, ensure_appended); otherwise
  the field fails instead of coming out with fewer candidates.
- Tests: the FD kernel against top_kernel on cubes of 100 and 48 units
  (partly filled planes) and 256, in both append forms, and on wgpu also
  with every plane's lane 0 appending alone (TOP_LANE0_OUT), on the test
  windows and the production fields' first slices (on CUDA one cube, as
  CubeCL lowers the lane id as AbsolutePos % 32); two test-only bits that
  make the append wrong (TOP_SHORT_TAKE, TOP_DUP_RANK) must fail a field
  and a partition's re-run with the matching error; a host unit test of
  top_complete. The test bits are comptime; production compiles without
  them.
