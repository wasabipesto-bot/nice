g2 port: --multi LIST, a client-like pipelined run over distinct super-units

- --multi LIST: LIST names super-unit directories (tops.bin + meta.txt; one may repeat). The CPU
  reference counts of every unit are computed up front in parallel. On the GPU: one exactness
  cycle (each unit's preparation checked against the host's records, order, class segments and
  top digits, and its counts against its CPU reference), a warm-up (--warm S), then --cycles C
  cycles over the units: per unit the host uploads its top masks, top segments and key digits
  (new buffers: CubeCL 0.10 has no write into an existing one) and queues its preparation and
  the full kernel with records; one synchronisation per cycle; every other buffer allocated once.
  Each unit's counters accumulate in its own buffer and must equal (1 + C) x its CPU reference.
  Reports the host's upload time and bytes per unit.
- scatter3_kernel: the scatter with the list length read on the device (hist2's count), launched
  for the list's capacity, so the host needs no parent count per unit.

The prototype's dumptops (dumpsu without bottoms and scalar join) staged the inputs on demoux;
its tops are byte-identical to the staged su64_712012851 and su64_391856203.
