## 2. The change

**Levels** (`exp/join-lean-pf`, host env `NICE_EXP_JOIN_PF` → comptime; cumulative; FX/MT/GM only at mid = 2, k ≥ 2,
i.e. every production shape and every test window but b10's k = 1):

| level | contents |
|---|---|
| 0 | the base |
| 1 | **FX**: the entry keeps `A·C` and `P` per power (4 words per slot instead of 2 packed); the batch load writes `dh = D·Q + ceil(D·R/b²)` with the seed flag in bit 0 (`dh` + 0–2); per pair `F = ah + p·dh`, `h = mul_hi(F, b)`, `g = mul_hi(F·b, b)` |
| 2 | + **MT**: membership by 64-bit shifts and six compares (three forms, below) |
| 3 | + **GM**: `t_glo`/`t_ghi` = the certificate gated by the seed flag, `t_dm` = plain `dh` (+2 KiB shared) |
| 4 | + **RC**: per-chunk clip words `s_cw` (set by `fetch_or` for tops with `rlo ≠ 0 ∨ rhi ≠ w`), the out-of-range pass bits of clipped tops cleared before the walk, `nand` by popcount; no residue test in the round |

**MT's forms** (`NICE_EXP_JOIN_MT`; SASS at b62 in §3):
- **0** (the scoping's): `hit = lo32(seen >> g2) | … ; (hit & 1) != 0`. NVVM narrows each `& 1` to an i1 and splits
  the test into four 64-bit compares (`LOP3 &1` + `ISETP` + `ISETP.EX` per digit): 15 instructions where 6 were
  planned. Level 2 was **60** instead of the predicted 48, worse than level 1.
- **1**: every digit mask of the join kernel held **bit-reversed** (digit d at bit 63 − d; the entry's in the wave
  load, the top's in the batch load: the AND, the transposed copy and the small-batch test are the same disjointness
  test under any labelling of the bits), so digit g is bit 63 of `seen << g`: `hi32(seen << g)` is one
  `SHF.L.U64.HI`, and the four OR into one sign test. The sign test first in the `||` chain compiled to `ISETP` +
  `SEL` + `SHF.R` + `LOP3` + `PRMT` + `ISETP` in front of the compares.
- **2** (shipped): the same with the sign test last (`i32(hit) < 0`): `ISETP.LT.OR` at the end of the compare chain.
  **3** (separate `if`) compiled identically.

**Other forms tried** (`NICE_EXP_JOIN_FXM=1`): FX's high words through u64 products instead of `mul_hi` (+2
instructions); `NICE_EXP_JOIN_RCV=0`: RC reading the clipped tops' ranges from the record instead of keeping
`t_rlo`/`t_rhi` in shared (same round; more spill-prone, below).

**The PR** (`P` = level 4, MT form 2, `mul_hi`, ranges in shared; no knobs): `walk_pf_lean` replaces `walk_pf` at
mid = 2, k ≥ 2; other shapes keep `walk_pf` and the packed words. RC applies to every shape. Shared memory: +2 KiB
(`t_glo`, `t_ghi`) and 32 B (`s_cw`): ~18 KiB per cube, still 5 cubes per SM (5 × 19 KiB ≤ 100 KiB). `w_f0` (= w) is a
new scalar argument of `join_kernel`.

- **Every level shrinks, and with MT in form 2 each is within 2–3 instructions of the prediction** (the gate's ~20 %).
  The ALU counts match exactly at levels 3 and 4 (24, 21).
- **The 2 left over at levels 1–4 are one artifact**: from level 1 on, ptxas no longer proves the warp converged at the
  walk's `VOTE.ANY` and plane barrier, and emits its fallback: `IMAD.MOV R, -1` + a never-taken `@!PT BRA` at the loop
  head, and at the tail a `BRA` over a dead `CALL` to the WARPSYNC routine (3 issued per round in all; the base's tail
  is `BSYNC`, `NOP`, `BRA`). Without it every level is the prediction to ±1 (57/49/45/39). The PTX around the vote is
  identical to the base's; offline edits of the CUDA source (`raw/box/cuexp/`, `cuexp.sh`) showed it does not come from
  the prefilter's body (a trivial body keeps it), from RC or from the batch load's new terms one at a time, and that
  dropping the plane barrier brings back a `YIELD` and costs more. Cause not found; the walk keeps the barrier.
- **Spills:** in some compiles ptxas lands on **40 registers with 28–44 B of spills** at b60 or b62 (`XL4`, `X3L4b`,
  `X3L4c`, `X3L4e/f`, `P3`, and the head's own passes: `P4` at b60, `P6` at b62); in others the same kernel takes 45–47
  registers and no spills (`P`, `P1`, `X3L4a`, `P4` at b62/b64, `P6` at b60/b64). The reason is in the source CubeCL emits: the
  join's shared memory is one `extern __shared__` dynamic array, so ptxas does not know it caps the kernel at 5 cubes per
  SM, aims at 6 (1536 threads, i.e. ≤ 40 registers), and takes the 40 whenever the spills it needs are few; which
  compile that is depends on CubeCL's per-process order of the shared arrays (the CUDA text differs between processes
  of one binary only in that order). The spills are never in the walk: they are work-item values (the work index,
  `lbase`) and four 64-bit values around the transposed copy, stored once per work item or batch and reloaded once per
  batch or wave, and the occupancy is 5 cubes per SM either way. The A/B's 3 rounds per field (separate processes,
  each compiling its own kernels) agree to 0.1–0.6 % on the PR's side (§6), so no run shows a spill cost. **The gate's
  "no spills" therefore holds for the timed binary's offline pass but not for every compile**; the base sits at
  exactly 40 registers at b60/b62 and never needs to spill. A launch bound of 5 blocks would settle it, but CubeCL
  0.10 emits only `__launch_bounds__(256)`.
- **Registers:** 40 → 45 at b60/b62 (the four FX words per slot instead of two packed), 47 at b64 as before. All ≤ 48,
  so occupancy is unchanged (5 cubes of 256 per SM, set by shared memory).
- **The 64-bit shifts:** `hi32(seen << g)` is one `SHF.L.U64.HI` per digit (verified, `P`'s SASS); FX's digits are
  `IMAD` + `IMAD.WIDE.U32` + `IMAD.HI.U32` per power (6 on the FMA pipe, 0 ALU), the reduction mod b² and both
  divisions gone.
- **The PR's round at b62** (`P`, loop at `0x3020`): loop control 6 (4 + the 2-instruction artifact), lane guard 4,
  pop 6, 3 `LDS` (`t_dm`, `t_glo`, `t_ghi`), 6 FMA (digits), 2 `LOP3` (seen), 4 `SHF.L.U64.HI` + 2 `LOP3` (hit),
  6 `ISETP.EQ.OR` + 1 `ISETP.LT.OR`, `BRA` = 41.
