# L1 field-certificate prune: A/B on an RTX 4090 (2026-10-29)

**Verdict: the prune wins at every base against new main (`2709d3f`) on this card. Ship it with EPT 2 at every base, replacing #205's EPT 6 from b62.**

- **1e16 A/B, the PR as pushed** (`26a8b15`, which keeps main's EPT 4/6): **×1.097 (b58), ×1.084 (b60), ×1.112 (b62), ×1.205 (b64)**. Spreads 0.1–2.4 % over 3 interleaved rounds.
- **With EPT 2** (a box-only override on the same tree): **×1.19 / 1.16 / 1.26 / 1.52**.
- **At production size** (1e14 fields, 20 per base): **×1.41 / 1.50 / 1.87 / 1.79 as pushed, ×1.55 / 1.55 / 1.97 / 2.08 with EPT 2**. Smaller fields know more certificate digits, so the prune removes 72–86 % of their bottoms.
- **NiceMark nice-only** (B as pushed): score **8086 → 9494 (×1.174)**. By scenario: b58_msd_weak ×1.152, b58_msd_strong ×1.330, b60_msd_weak ×1.075, b62_msd_weak ×1.126. Every scenario took the join.
- **EPT matrix** (prune on the old base, 2/3/4/6 × 3 rounds): **EPT 2 is best at every base**, and the order 2 < 3 < 4 < 6 holds in every round. EPT 2 beats EPT 4 by ×1.07–1.13 and EPT 6 by ×1.15–1.27.
- **Exactness:**
  - COMPARE: found 0 = 0 at every base.
  - Survivors and checks are identical to the last digit between main, prune and prune + EPT 2 at 1e14.
  - The CPU prune test passes on the rebased tree.
  - The bottoms removed are the same as yesterday's: #203's closure does not change the field certificate at these fields.
- **Why the 1e16 gains are below the 3080's (×1.15 / 1.15 / 1.42 / 1.64): it is not the card.** The old pair on this 4090 gives ×1.14 / 1.14 / 1.37 / 1.58. The causes are new main's EPT 6 at b62/b64, and main's own gains (×1.10–1.29 over the old base), which shrink the share the prune removes.
- **The 3080's stage-overrun flag does not reproduce on new main.** Prune and main counters agree to 7 figures at 1e16.

## 7. Exactness on this card

- **`NICE_TEST_JOIN_COMPARE=1`** on the prune tree (`26a8b15`), 2 fields of 1e14 per base at the A/B starts (`ab/compare_prune.log`): the join and the stride pipeline agree, **found 0 = 0 at b58/60/62/64**, test ok. As yesterday, these fields have no nice numbers, so this alone is weak.
- **Stronger evidence from the timing runs:** at 1e14 (section 3), survivors and full checks are identical to the last digit between main, prune and prune + EPT 2 on every base and round (9 runs × 4 bases × 20 fields). At 1e16 they agree to 7 figures, within each tree's own run-to-run overrun noise.
- **CPU prune test** (`cpu_join::tests::the_field_prune_drops_exactly_the_bottoms_that_meet_the_certificate`, on the new-main prune tree with a box-only fields knob): passes on all 16 fields below, so the pruned `bpre` and bottom lists equal the unpruned ones filtered by the certificate.
- **CUDA parity tests on `26a8b15`** (`ab/parity.log`), all passed:
  - `cubecl_cuda_join_matches_the_cpu_reference`: every window, tight layouts, slices and refused layouts agree with the unpruned reference;
  - `..._on_a_dense_field` (`NICE_TEST_JOIN_DENSE=64`, b58 1e15): 0 survivors overran the stage;
  - `..._on_a_whole_field` (b42; `fc = 0` there, so a regression check only): 417,789,835 join survivors, 16,879,877 checked, no hits.
- **Not finished:** the dense test on the b64 1e16 A/B field (`NICE_TEST_JOIN_DENSE_FIELD`, the 3080's overrun flag). Its 64-partition CPU side did not finish within my 300 s timeout.

**The card is not why the new-main gains are smaller.** On the old pair the 4090 reproduces the 3080 within 1–4 %. The gap comes from new main itself:

- **#205's EPT 6 at b62/b64** made unpruned main faster and pruned buckets slower (EPT 6 costs ×1.15 / ×1.27 against EPT 2 with the prune, section 5). With EPT 2, b62/b64 recover to ×1.26 / ×1.52 (section 2).
- **Main's other gains** (#203 closure, #206 batches) make main ×1.20 / 1.10 / 1.29 / 1.26 faster than the old base here (29.74 → 24.78 s at b58, etc.). They shrink the survivor and launch side the prune does not touch, so the bottom side it removes is a smaller share: at b58/b60 the prune gives ×1.10 / 1.08 on new main against ×1.14 / 1.14 on the old base.
- Combined, new main + prune + EPT 2 against the old base on this card (cross-run, same box): ×1.44 / 1.27 / 1.62 / 1.90.

Other comparisons with the 3080:

- **Absolute speed:** the 4090 at 330 W runs the old base 2.3× (b58, b60, b62) to 2.4× (b64) faster than the 3080 (69.36 / 45.26 / 26.98 / 83.42 s there).
- **EPT:** the same ranking (2 < 3 < 4 < 6 here, 2 < 4 and 1 worse there), with nearly the same ratios.
- **Stage overruns:** on the old pair the prune moves survivors into the unprefiltered path at b62/b64 as on the 3080 (b64: −1.9e8 survivors, +1.75e8 checks; 2.2e-4 of them). **On new main it does not:** main and prune counters agree to 7 figures at 1e16 and exactly at 1e14. So the 3080's flag about the dense-test limit at b64 likely does not apply to the PR as rebased. The b64 dense test itself timed out (section 7).
