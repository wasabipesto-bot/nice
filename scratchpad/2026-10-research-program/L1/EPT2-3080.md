# `pr/join-ept-2`: two bottom entries per GPU thread, on an RTX 3080 (2026-10-29)

**Two entries per thread win at every base, at both field sizes, on top of the prune.** Against `pr/field-cert-prune`:
- **1e14** (production size, 20 fields per base, the branch itself): **×1.076 (b58), ×1.037 (b60), ×1.117 (b62), ×1.220 (b64)**.
- **1e16** (two fields per base, 3 rounds): **×1.091 / ×1.059 / ×1.167 / ×1.262**. These rounds used the PR tree with a box-only `NICE_EXP_JOIN_EPT=2` override, interleaved with the main/PR A/B; the same constant reaches the kernel. One more round with `pr/join-ept-2`'s own binary (`cb38b91`) agrees with them within 2 % (×1.094 / 1.071 / 1.143 / 1.258 against the PR's medians).
- Against upstream main: **×1.69 / 1.77 / 2.53 / 2.65 at 1e14** and **×1.218 / 1.163 / 1.349 / 1.577 at 1e16**.
- No regression anywhere. Spreads are 0.2–4.1 %, and the smallest gain (b60 1e14, ×1.037) is above the spread of either tree (0.9 / 2.4 %).
- Counters are identical to the last digit with and without EPT 2 at 1e14 (and to main's). At 1e16 they agree to within the stage overrun.
- With the 4090 (coordinator's AB-4090.md: EPT 2 < 3 < 4 < 6 in every round; prune + EPT 2 vs main ×1.19 / 1.16 / 1.26 / 1.52 at 1e16, ×1.55 / 1.55 / 1.97 / 2.08 at 1e14), that is two NVIDIA cards agreeing. The A100 and the RX 9070 XT (wgpu) are unmeasured.

## The branch

`pr/join-ept-2` on the fork (`wasabipesto-bot/nice`), stacked on `pr/field-cert-prune` (`941e39e`): one commit, **`cb38b91`** "overlap join: two bottom entries per GPU thread at every base".
- `cubecl_join.rs`: `entries_per_thread(base)` (#205: 6 from base 62, 4 below) becomes `const ENTRIES_PER_THREAD: u32 = 2`, with a comment saying why: since the field prune a bucket holds 3–4× fewer entries (about 280–420 on 1e16 fields at b58–64, fewer at 1e14), so 1,024- and 1,536-entry waves ran mostly empty lanes.
- `CHANGELOG.md`: #205's unreleased line ("six bottom entries instead of four") becomes "two bottom entries instead of four (six from base 62), which fits the pruned bottom lists".
- fmt-clean on ivory; not separately clippy-checked (the change removes a function and uses a const in its one call site).
