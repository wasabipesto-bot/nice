How to read this

Colour is where a piece runs: host (the client's driver thread), device kernels, the CPU reference on a host thread, the coordinator script and the wire, and today's overlap join. A gold REUSED badge marks production code used unchanged; everything else in the two left lanes is new.

Shape is role: rounded box = step; hexagon = decision; double rounded box with ⟳ = loop; box with a top bar and cut corners = device kernel; curved box = data built once and kept.

Badges give how often a piece runs: PROC once per process, FIELD once per field, /64 once per 64 slices, SLICE, BATCH, GROUP (16 slices). ACC marks a piece that writes an execution account, CHECKS one that compares them.

Bars are the share of a slice's time (stage totals, drawn on the stage's main shape). The RX 9070 XT profile was measured by cumulative timing stops on field 77777; its tops and parents ran off the operating point (the card idles on those stages alone), so those two are too high and the main kernel's share too low. Overlap-lane shares are derived, not measured per stage.

Line numbers are into dev/mo-clean at 66305ada for the middle-out lanes, and origin/main at 974d7194 for the overlap lane; each was checked by printing the line.
